# Puppy and Kitten Care

## Feeding schedule

**Puppies:**
- 6-12 weeks: 4 meals per day
- 3-6 months: 3 meals per day
- 6-12 months: 2-3 meals per day
- After 12 months (small breeds) or 18-24 months (large breeds): switch to adult food, 2 meals per day

Use puppy-specific food during growth — adult food doesn't have the right calcium-to-phosphorus ratio. Large-breed puppies need large-breed puppy food (different growth rate to prevent joint problems).

**Kittens:**
- Under 6 weeks: typically still nursing or bottle-fed every 2-4 hours
- 6-12 weeks: 4 meals per day of kitten food
- 3-6 months: 3 meals per day
- 6+ months: transition to 2 meals per day; switch to adult food at 12 months

## Vaccination schedule

**Puppies** (core vaccines):
- 6-8 weeks: DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parvo, parainfluenza)
- 10-12 weeks: DHPP booster
- 14-16 weeks: DHPP final booster + rabies
- Annual or 3-year boosters thereafter, depending on vet protocol

**Kittens**:
- 6-8 weeks: FVRCP (rhinotracheitis, calicivirus, panleukopenia)
- 10-12 weeks: FVRCP booster
- 14-16 weeks: FVRCP final + rabies
- FeLV (feline leukemia) recommended for cats with outdoor access

Until full vaccination, puppies should not visit dog parks or walk in heavily-trafficked dog areas. Carrying them to controlled social experiences (a friend's vaccinated dog, a quiet park bench) is safer.

## House training

**Puppies**: take outside immediately after waking, eating, drinking, and playing. A puppy can typically hold their bladder for one hour per month of age (a 3-month-old puppy needs a potty break every 3 hours). Reward immediately when they go outside — timing matters more than treat size.

Crate training helps because dogs naturally avoid soiling their sleeping space. The crate should be just large enough to stand up, turn around, and lie down — too large and they'll eliminate in one corner.

**Kittens**: most use a litter box instinctively. Place one in a quiet, accessible location and show them the box after meals.

## Sleep needs

Puppies and kittens sleep 18-20 hours a day. This is normal. Letting them sleep undisturbed is important for development. Wake-and-play cycles every 2-3 hours work better than expecting all-day activity.

## Socialization window

Critical socialization windows:
- Puppies: 3-14 weeks
- Kittens: 2-9 weeks

During these windows, positive exposure to people, other animals, sounds, surfaces, and handling shapes lifelong temperament. Missed socialization is hard to fully recover.
