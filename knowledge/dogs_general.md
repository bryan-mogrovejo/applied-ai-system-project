# Dog Care Basics

## Exercise needs

Most adult dogs need 30 to 120 minutes of physical activity per day, split into two or more sessions. Working and herding breeds (Border Collies, Australian Shepherds, Huskies) lean toward the high end and benefit from mental work like puzzle feeders. Toy and brachycephalic breeds (Pugs, French Bulldogs) need much less and can overheat easily.

A common rule of thumb: walk a dog at least twice a day — once in the morning and once in the evening. Morning walks before breakfast can help with digestion and reduce begging behavior. Evening walks help burn off energy before bedtime.

## Feeding

Adult dogs typically eat twice a day, roughly 12 hours apart. Puppies under 6 months need 3-4 smaller meals. Always provide fresh water alongside food. Avoid free-feeding (leaving food out all day) for most breeds, as it makes weight management harder and obscures appetite changes that signal illness.

## Walking schedule

A standard daily layout for a healthy adult dog:
- Morning: 20-30 minute walk before or just after breakfast
- Midday: short potty break (5-10 min) if the dog is alone for long periods
- Evening: 30+ minute walk, ideally with some off-leash or sniffing time
- Bedtime: brief potty break before sleep

Skipping walks for more than a day often leads to destructive indoor behavior, especially in young or high-energy dogs.

## Mental enrichment

Sniffing on walks counts as mental work — let dogs pause and explore, don't drag them. Puzzle feeders, training sessions, and chew toys help prevent boredom on rainy days when outdoor exercise is limited.
