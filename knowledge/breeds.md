# Breed-Specific Care Notes

Breed differences are real and matter for daily care. The same task list won't fit every dog or cat.

## Dogs

**Border Collie / Australian Shepherd / Working breeds**: need 2+ hours of vigorous exercise daily plus mental work (training, herding ball, scent work). Without it, they invent jobs — usually destructive ones. Not suitable for sedentary households.

**Husky / Malamute**: high prey drive, bred to run. Need fenced yards or leashed walks — they bolt and don't reliably recall. Heavy seasonal shedding ("blowing coat") in spring and fall.

**Labrador / Golden Retriever**: high food motivation, prone to obesity. Strict portion control is essential. Need swimming or fetch-style exercise; a slow walk doesn't drain their energy.

**French Bulldog / Pug / Brachycephalic breeds**: short snouts make breathing inefficient. Avoid exercise in heat (above 75°F / 24°C). Walk early morning or late evening in summer. Prone to overheating, snoring, and breathing surgeries.

**Poodle / Doodle / Bichon**: hair (not fur) requires professional grooming every 4-6 weeks plus daily brushing to prevent painful matting.

**Dachshund / Corgi (long-backed breeds)**: prone to back injuries (intervertebral disc disease). Discourage jumping off furniture; use ramps or steps for couches and beds.

**Great Dane / Saint Bernard / Giant breeds**: short lifespans (7-10 years), prone to bloat (gastric torsion — a fatal emergency). Feed two smaller meals instead of one large meal, avoid exercise immediately after eating.

**German Shepherd**: prone to hip dysplasia. Avoid over-exercising puppies on hard surfaces during growth.

## Cats

**Persian / Himalayan**: long coat needs daily brushing. Brachycephalic face causes tear staining and breathing issues.

**Maine Coon / Norwegian Forest Cat**: large, heavy cats prone to joint and heart issues. Feed measured portions to prevent obesity.

**Siamese / Oriental**: highly vocal and social. Become depressed if left alone all day — consider getting two.

**Ragdoll**: extremely docile, do not defend themselves well. Strictly indoor cats.

**Sphynx (hairless)**: need weekly baths to remove skin oils that would otherwise be absorbed by fur. Sensitive to cold and sunburn.

**Bengal**: high energy, almost dog-like. Need vertical space, puzzle feeders, and active play. Not a low-maintenance cat.

## When breed knowledge matters

Use breed information as a starting point, not a verdict. Individual personality, training, and health vary widely within breeds. A "high energy" breed adopted as a senior may be perfectly happy with short walks.
